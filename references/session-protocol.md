# Full Session Protocols and Monitor Rules

## Three Classes of Sessions

### Class C – Training

- Monitor is knowledgeable of the site.
- Limited intra-session feedback is allowed.
- Results are not used for proof-of-principle.

### Class B – Confirmation / Evaluation

- Monitor is blind to the site.
- Feedback given only after the session is complete and the summary is written.

### Class A – Operational

- Monitor is blind in the majority of cases.
- No feedback during or immediately after the session.
- Data are passed to analysts for use.

## Monitor Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. Deliver the coordinate once, clearly, and stop talking.
2. Never interpret, never suggest, never ask “what else do you see?”
3. Never supply content of any kind mid-session.
4. Observe only. Note timestamps if required.
5. At the formal end of session, instruct the viewer to produce a clean stage-by-stage summary.
6. Only after the summary is complete may ground-truth feedback be given.

## Viewer Rules

1. Declare personal inclemencies and advance visuals before the coordinate.
2. Pen tip on paper is the only readiness signal.
3. Ideogram first. Always.
4. Declare every AOL the instant it appears.
5. Declare AI and EI the instant they appear.
6. Never force data. “No signal” is a valid report.
7. Produce a clean summary after the session ends.

## Environment

Ideal: acoustic-tiled, featureless, homogeneously colored room with minimal visual and auditory stimulation.

Acceptable substitute: quiet room, low lighting, no windows with active views, no conversation, no music, no phone.

## Coordinate Formats

- Full geographic: degrees, minutes, seconds
- Encrypted numerical string

## Ending the Session

The viewer or monitor may end the session when:

- The signal line goes quiet for a sustained period.
- The viewer reports exhaustion of the aperture.
- A pre-agreed time limit is reached.

After ending, the viewer writes the formal summary organized by stage.
